# Hifz Revision

A revision app for huffāẓ: recite from memory while the app listens and catches slips, then relearn weak āyāt on a spaced schedule.

- Listening runs **on your phone** — audio never leaves the device.
- Your weak list and progress are saved in your phone's browser only (not in this repository). Use *Save backup* on the Today screen.
